# Decision log

Written by the owner, in their own words, one entry per week (see "Yours to do by hand" in
`docs/v2/plan-to-tet-2027.md`). Short is fine. These entries become interview answers and blog posts.

Each entry answers four questions:
1. **What did I build or measure?**
2. **What did I decide, and what were the alternatives?**
3. **What did the numbers show?** (with the uncertainty, if there is any)
4. **What would I do differently, or what don't I understand yet?**
